Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ankit Raj Vishwakarma, who operates Lone Star Storm Repair at www.lonestarstormrepair.com ("we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit our website, call our phone number or submit a request. By using our website or contacting us, you agree to this policy.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The data controller for this website is Ankit Raj Vishwakarma, operating as Lone Star Storm Repair. You can contact us at help@lonestarstormrepair.com or by phone at (214) 555-0100.

2. What we do

Lone Star Storm Repair is an independent advertising and referral service. We connect homeowners and property owners with independent roofing and restoration companies. We are not a contractor, insurance agent or public adjuster.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, phone number, email address and ZIP code or property location
  • A description of your storm damage, such as hail, wind, a leak or flooding
  • Anything else you tell us when you call, text, email or fill in a form

Information collected when you call us

  • Your phone number, the time and length of the call and which tracking number you dialed
  • A recording of the call, where permitted, and notes about your request
  • The outcome of the referral, as reported by the contractor we connected you with

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, approximate location, browser, device type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, time on site and the page that referred you
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see section 8)

4. How we use your information

  • To understand your request and connect you with a roofing or restoration company that serves your area
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request
  • To track, measure and verify calls and referrals, and to pay or be paid for them
  • To operate, secure and improve our website, including preventing spam and fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations and to enforce our terms

5. Call recording

Calls to our phone number may be recorded and monitored for quality, training, record-keeping and billing. By continuing a call after you are told it may be recorded, you consent to the recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call or use the request form instead.

6. How we share your information

  • Contractors. We share your name, phone number, location and the details of your request with the roofing or restoration company we match you with, so that they can contact you. These companies are independent and handle your information under their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers. We use vendors for website hosting, form handling, call tracking and recording, analytics and email. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information to comply with law, respond to lawful requests or protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • Business changes. If the business is sold or transferred, your information may be transferred with it.

Sale of personal information. We may be paid by contractors when we connect a qualified call or request. Under some state privacy laws, sharing your information with a contractor in exchange for payment can be considered a "sale" of personal data. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers and we do not use it for unrelated third-party advertising. You can opt out of any sale using the instructions in section 10.

7. Calls and text messages

When you provide your phone number and submit a request or call us, you agree that we and the contractor we match you with may contact you about your request by phone, text message or email, including with automated technology. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a text to opt out, or tell us on a call to stop contacting you.

8. Cookies, analytics and third-party services

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, to understand how visitors use it and to measure call and form conversions. Our pages also load content from third parties, including Google Fonts and Google Maps embeds, which may collect your IP address and device information under their own privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working. If we use analytics or advertising cookies, we will describe them here.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, resolve disputes, keep business and call records and meet legal obligations. We delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including Texas under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act and California under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and request access to it
  • Correct inaccuracies and request deletion
  • Obtain a portable copy of the information you gave us
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising and profiling
  • Appeal a decision if we decline your request

To exercise these rights, or to opt out of the sale of your information, email help@lonestarstormrepair.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and the phone number or email you used with us. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within the time required by law, generally within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No website or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other sites, including contractors' sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be posted on this page.
